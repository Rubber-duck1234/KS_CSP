Helldivers 2


What it is:

This game is absolutly amazing. Helldivers 2 is the second game of the Helldivers series and takes place around 140 years from today. In the game, you are a Helldiver, an elite soldier that travles to planets to "liberate" them from the "evil" clutches of the other non playable factions. To liberate a planet, you work with other plays to compleat missions, by compleating them you advance the liberation progress and when at 100 the planet is now part of the "super earth federation". Its not just liberating plantes it also tactical, sometimes the enemy forces move the Helldivers into a fork, making which planet to defend a choice having an impact on the galactic war. There are three oposing factions, they are the terminids, automatons, and illuminite. The game is persistant meaning that it goes on all day every day and doesn't reset. You are given major orders from the devs addvancing the war and giving direction to the players. In the game you can play mutiplayer or just by yourself, but I will say it is so much more fun with firends "liberating by your side" as the game likes to say.


The three oposing factions:

There are three factions that you fight against, no you can not play as them. The first and oldest is the terminids. They are bugs, they were the first faction when the game first lanched and are my personal favorite to play against. Their units mainly charge you and meele you but a few can spit projectials and hit you from far away. The second is the automatons, They are a robot/cyborg faction. They are the second faction put into the game and the robots actually came before the cyborgs, but they are still the same faction. Their units also use projectials but they move faster then the terminids. Then the third is the illuminate. Imagine alians from the movies but put armor on them and make them a little taller and then you have an image of what they are. They are a small but powerfull faction that is the comunitys and my, least favorite to fight, but are also very interesting. Unlike the other two factions, the illuminate are more mysterious in their origins, the terminids are just larger mutated bugs, and the cyborgs and automatons are a split faction that came from the "super earth" faction, which is you, but the illuminate came from galaxys that we havent even seen.
