The Bonneville Salt Flats


The salt flats came from Lake Bonneville that once covered one-third of Utah, and now cover over 30,000 acres of land with salt. The Great Salt Lake is what remains of the large lake. The flats do have small ponds and marshy areas where some small plants and animals live. To the right of this is a list for why you should visit.


This one changes when you hover over it.


  • Salt. who doesn't want to see 147 million tons of it.
  • Landscape Pictures
  • Racing, lots of races happen there.
  • Star gazing and sun sets/rises, You can see the stars well there as well as sun rises and sun sets.
